# FestOS Volunteer & Recruit Training Manual

Welcome to the FestOS Committee! This manual is your quick-start guide to operating the digital panels during the festival. 

No matter which desk you are assigned to, **you are the face of the festival**. Your actions are recorded in the system's Audit Log under your name and roll number, so please handle participant data carefully.

---

## 1. On-Spot Registration Desk (`/desk`)
**Overview:** You are the frontline for attendees walking in to register for events using Cash or UPI. 
**Your Gig (Step-by-Step for a successful registration):**
*   **Log In:** Use the On-Spot panel password. Enter your Student Name and Roll No in the Operator Check-In modal.
*   **Select Event:** Choose the event the participant wants to join from the dropdown.
*   **Fill Details:** Enter the Lead Attendee's Name, Email, Phone, and College. *(Double-check the email! This is where the QR ticket is sent).*
*   **Team Members:** If it's a team event, add the names of all other team members.
*   **Collect Payment:**
    *   **Cash:** Collect the exact cash amount shown on screen. Select the "Cash" payment method.
    *   **UPI:** Ask the participant to scan the Razorpay QR or send money to the official UPI. You do NOT need to manually enter the Transaction ID if they pay via the on-screen Razorpay QR; the system links it automatically. 
*   **Submit:** Click submit. A success message will appear, and tickets will automatically be emailed to the participant.
*   **Repeat:** The form will clear automatically. Smile and move to the next person in line!

## 2. Gate Security & Check-In (`/checkin`)
**Overview:** You control the entry points to the festival or specific events, ensuring only valid ticket-holders enter.
**Your Gig:**
*   **Scan QR Code:** Use a barcode scanner or your phone camera to scan the participant's Ticket QR code.
*   **Verify Identity:** The screen will show the participant's Name, College, and Event. Ask to see their College ID card and verify the names match.
*   **Check-In:** Click the "Check-In" button. The system will mark the ticket as used in the database.
*   **Fraud Prevention:** If the screen flashes RED (Ticket already used or invalid), politely deny entry and direct them to the Help Desk.
*   **Tagging:** Hand over the festival wristband or stamp their hand, then let them pass.

## 3. Stage & AV Committee (`/stage`)
**Overview:** You manage the audio tracks, background videos, and sound cues for performers on stage.
**Your Gig:**
*   **Monitor the Lineup:** Look at the roster of upcoming performers for the current event.
*   **Check Audio Links:** Verify that the performer has uploaded a valid Google Drive link for their audio track.
*   **Update Tracks:** If a performer hands you a pen drive at the last minute, upload the file to the master Drive folder and paste the new link into their record.
*   **Coordinate with Sound Engineer:** Read the "Track Notes" (e.g., *"Start track at 0:45"*, *"Blackout before song ends"*) to the sound engineer exactly as the performer requested.

## 4. Registration & Invitation (R&I) (`/admin`)
**Overview:** You oversee all competitive event registrations and act as the primary help desk for participant issues.
**Your Gig:**
*   **Resolve Issues:** Search for participants who haven't received their tickets or misspelled their email address.
*   **Edit Records:** (If permitted by Super Admin) Fix typos in participant names, update college names, or correct phone numbers.
*   **Monitor Numbers:** Keep an eye on total registrations for capacity-limited events.
*   **Export Rosters:** Download the CSV of participants and hand it to the respective judging panels before an event begins.

## 5. Informalz Committee (`/informalz`)
**Overview:** You run the casual, fun events and mini-games that aren't strictly competitive.
**Your Gig:**
*   **Issue Day Passes:** Participants buy a Day 1 (₹150), Day 2 (₹150), or Both Days (₹250) pass. Issue these just like On-Spot registrations.
*   **Track Participation:** Verify a participant's Day Pass at the entrance of your specific mini-game (e.g., Ring Toss, Dartboard).
*   **Enforce Rules:** Ensure participants only play the games covered by their specific pass (a Day 1 pass cannot play Day 2 games).

## 6. Cultural & Niche Committees (Music, Dance, Theatre, Fashion, Literary, Gaming)
**Overview:** You run the actual competitions. Each committee has its own isolated panel (e.g., `/committee/music`, `/committee/gaming`).
**Your Gig:**
*   **View Roster:** See exactly who is competing in your category and their payment status.
*   **Call Participants:** Use the phone numbers in the roster to call teams that are late to the staging area.
*   **Check-In for Competition:** Mark teams as "Present" at your desk so the judges know who is actually performing.
*   **Coordinate with Stage:** Tell the Stage Committee who is up next so they can queue the correct audio track.

## 7. Super Admin & Management (`/super-admin`, `/management`)
**Overview:** Reserved for core team and university officials.
**Your Gig:**
*   **Management Panel:** View-only access to see how much revenue is collected and how many students are participating. 
*   **Super Admin:** Can edit events, create new ones, grant edit access to other panels, and monitor the live Audit Log of every action taken by every volunteer across the entire campus.
